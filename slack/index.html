01 — What it does

The question gets asked in Slack. Answer it there.

The Monday ritual is export a CSV, paste it somewhere, squint at it, then paraphrase the answer back into the channel. Bratrax skips to the last step.

Ask the way you'd ask a colleague

No query language, no hunting for a saved report. And the obvious follow-up works — “and just for Facebook?” — because the thread keeps its context.

Charts and tables, native to Slack

Answers come back as real Slack charts and sortable tables, not screenshots of a dashboard — so they stay readable on a phone, which is where half of these questions get asked. Every answer carries a button through to the dashboard it came from.

Nothing to learn on day one

Open a direct message with it and it offers the first few questions. Or invite it to a channel and mention it there — it answers when spoken to and never posts on its own.

05 — What it can't do

Read-only, by design.

  • It can't change anything. The assistant can query your metrics and read your dashboards. It cannot write to your data, your store, or your ad accounts.
  • It only sees channels it's in. Channels it's been invited to, and DMs sent to it. Nothing else in your workspace.
  • It doesn't store your messages. Your question is read to answer it and isn't written to our database.
  • It answers for one store. A workspace is bound to one Bratrax account. Multi-store operators connect one workspace per store.
  • It never posts unprompted. No alerts, no scheduled digests. It speaks when spoken to.
  • Disconnect any time from Settings → Slack, or by removing the app in Slack. Either revokes the token and deletes the connection.

Full detail in the privacy policy and the Slack FAQ.

Before you rely on it

It can be wrong. Answers are generated by a large language model. It can misread a question, reach for the wrong date range, or state a number with more confidence than it has earned. Every answer carries a link into the dashboard it came from — check anything you're about to act on.

The split-view assistant panel needs a paid Slack plan. That's Slack's requirement, not ours. On a free plan you can still mention @bratrax in any channel or send it a direct message — it answers the same way.
