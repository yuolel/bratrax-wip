# Customer email — Bratrax for Slack

Goes to the **whole list**, not just paying customers. Two jobs at once:

- **Already paying** — get curious enough to switch Slack on.
- **Not set up yet** — see something they want and go build an account for it.

That means the body sells the product, not the upgrade, and the fork between
the two audiences happens once, at the bottom, in the CTA. A reader who is
already on Bratrax never has to wade through "what Bratrax is"; a reader who
isn't never feels the email was written past them.

## Subject line

**Skip the dashboard. Ask Bratrax in Slack.**

Benefit first, mechanic second, and short enough to survive a phone. The benefit
is the one everybody on the list feels whether or not they pay us yet: the
answer without the trip to go and get it.

Alternates worth testing:

| Subject | Leads with |
|---|---|
| *"Which campaigns lost money last week?" Ask in Slack.* | A real question they have had this month |
| *Your ROAS answer, in the thread* | The place the answer arrives |
| *The afternoon question, answered in a sentence* | Time saved — vaguest of the four, strongest curiosity |

## Body

---

**Ask in Slack. Get the answer in the thread.**

Bratrax now works inside Slack. Mention **@bratrax** in any channel and ask the
question you'd normally open a dashboard for. The answer comes back right there
— the number, what moved it, and a chart everyone can read at the same time.

![A ranked campaign table answered in a Slack thread](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuolel/bratrax-wip/bratrax-com-static/assets/slack-listing-images/01-campaign-table.png)

No screenshot. No *"give me a minute."* Nobody leaving the conversation to go
and look.

It's built for the questions that used to take an afternoon:

- *Which campaigns lost money last week?*
- *What's my real CAC once returns are out?*
- *How much revenue is still landing in Direct?*
- *Did the email flow beat the paid campaign?*

Ask a follow-up and it remembers what you were on about — *"and just for
Facebook?"* works.

Every answer carries a link into the dashboard behind it, so anything you're
about to act on can be checked in a click. It can pull any number in your
account in seconds, and it can't change a single one.

**Already on Bratrax? It takes about a minute to switch on.**

Go to **Settings → Slack** and follow the instructions on screen.

![The Slack tab in Bratrax settings, with the connect button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuolel/bratrax-wip/bratrax-com-static/assets/slack-listing-images/email-connect-slack-dark.png)

**[Connect your workspace →](https://bratrax.com/settings/slack)**

It's included in every plan. No Slack tier, no per-seat charge.

**Not set up yet?** **[Start here](https://bratrax.com/signup)** — connect your
store and the ad platforms you spend on, and the Slack assistant is waiting for
you from day one.

More on what it reads and what it won't do at
**[bratrax.com/slack](https://bratrax.com/slack)**.

— Yuliya

## Notes for setting it up

- **Primary CTA** is `https://bratrax.com/settings/slack`. For a logged-in
  customer it lands on the exact tab with the connect button. For everyone else
  it bounces to login, which is the right place for them to be anyway.
- **Second CTA** is `https://bratrax.com/signup` — confirm that's the URL you
  want new accounts landing on before sending.
- **Third link** is `https://bratrax.com/slack` for anyone who wants to read
  first.
- **Both images** are live on the static branch, so the raw URLs above work
  as-is. Uploading them into beehiiv directly is better for deliverability; the
  files are in `assets/slack-listing-images/`.
- If only one image fits, keep the first. It's the one doing the selling —
  the settings screenshot is instruction, not persuasion.
