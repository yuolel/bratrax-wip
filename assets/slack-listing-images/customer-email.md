**Bratrax now works inside Slack.**

Mention **@bratrax** in any channel and ask the question you'd normally open a
dashboard for. The answer comes back in the thread — the number, what moved it,
and a chart everyone can read at the same time.

![A ranked campaign table answered in a Slack thread](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuolel/bratrax-wip/bratrax-com-static/assets/slack-listing-images/01-campaign-table.png)

It's for the questions that used to take an afternoon:

- *Which campaigns lost money last week?*
- *What's my real CAC once returns are out?*
- *How much revenue is still landing in Direct?*
- *Did the email flow beat the paid campaign?*

Ask a follow-up and it remembers what you were on about — *"and just for
Facebook?"* works. Every answer carries a link into the dashboard behind it, so
anything you're about to act on can be checked in a click.

It can pull any number in your account in seconds, and it can't change a single
one — not your data, not your store, not your ad accounts. It only sees the
channels you invite it into.

**Turning it on takes about a minute.**

Open **Settings → Slack** in Bratrax and click connect. Slack asks you to
approve it, you land back in Bratrax, and it's on. No keys to copy, nothing to
configure.

![The Slack tab in Bratrax settings, with the connect button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuolel/bratrax-wip/bratrax-com-static/assets/slack-listing-images/email-connect-slack-dark.png)

**[Connect your workspace →](https://bratrax.com/settings/slack)**

It's included in every plan. No Slack tier, no per-seat charge, no bill that
grows with the questions your team asks.

More detail — what it reads, what it won't do — at
**[bratrax.com/slack](https://bratrax.com/slack)**.

— Yuliya
