Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: August 13, 2026  ·  Version: 1.0

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Bratrax Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between Inceptly LLC, a Delaware limited liability company doing business as Bratrax ("Bratrax," "we," "us," or "Processor"), and the customer that has entered into the Agreement ("Customer," "you," or "Controller"). It governs Bratrax's processing of Personal Data on Customer's behalf and replaces the commitment in Section 5.8 of the Agreement to execute a separate data processing agreement on request.

No signature required

  • This DPA is already in force. It is incorporated by reference into the Agreement. By accepting the Agreement or using the Service, Customer enters into this DPA. Neither party needs to sign, countersign, or return a copy.
  • It includes the Standard Contractual Clauses. The European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and the Swiss amendments are incorporated and completed by the Annexes below. Customer does not need to execute them separately.
  • If your procurement process requires a countersigned copy, email legal@bratrax.com and we will provide one. A countersigned copy carries the same terms as this page; signing changes nothing except the paperwork.
  • Bratrax is a processor for your store and advertising data, and a controller for the account and website data described in Section 2.3 of our Privacy Policy.
  • Current subprocessors are listed in Annex III. Security measures are in Annex II. Both are maintained on this page.

1. Definitions

  • "Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data contained within Customer Data (as defined in the Agreement) that Bratrax Processes on Customer's behalf under the Agreement.
  • "Data Protection Laws" means all laws applicable to the Processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP, the CCPA, and other U.S. state privacy laws.
  • "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679. "UK GDPR" means the GDPR as incorporated into the law of the United Kingdom by the Data Protection Act 2018. "Swiss FADP" means the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.
  • "CCPA" means the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.
  • "Personal Data," "Controller," "Processor," "Data Subject," "Processing," "Personal Data Breach," and "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "Processing" and "Process" are construed accordingly.
  • "SCCs" means the Standard Contractual Clauses annexed to European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.
  • "UK Addendum" means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018, version B1.0.
  • "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Bratrax to Process Customer Personal Data.

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement.

2. Roles of the Parties

2.1 Customer as Controller, Bratrax as Processor

With respect to Customer Personal Data, Customer is the Controller and Bratrax is the Processor. This covers all data Customer connects to the Service from Connected Platforms — store data, order and customer records, advertising and campaign data, email and SMS engagement data — together with data collected by the Bratrax storefront integration on Customer's own web properties, and the reports, dashboards, and attribution outputs Bratrax generates from that data.

Storefront tracking. Bratrax collects attribution data through a first-party integration that Customer installs on its own storefront and controls. The form of that integration depends on Customer's commerce platform — it may be an application extension that subscribes to the platform's own standard event set, or a plugin or script that Customer installs and can remove — but the arrangement is the same in every case. Customer decides whether to deploy the integration, decides the purpose for which it is deployed, configures its own campaign tagging in its own advertising accounts, and may remove it at any time. Where the integration runs on Customer's own systems it may also write attribution identifiers back into Customer's order records, so that the attribution evidence remains in Customer's possession. Bratrax Processes the resulting data solely to produce Customer's own attribution reporting, and for no other purpose.

2.2 Customer as Processor

Where Customer is itself a Processor acting on behalf of a third-party controller — for example, an agency operating the Service for a client — Bratrax is a Subprocessor. In that case, references in this DPA to Customer's instructions mean instructions permitted by the relevant controller, and Customer warrants that it has the authority to give those instructions and to enter into this DPA on that controller's behalf. Module Three of the SCCs applies in place of Module Two.

2.3 Bratrax as Controller

Bratrax acts as an independent Controller for a narrow, separate category of data: account registration details of Customer's administrators and users (name, email address, company, role), billing metadata received from our payment processor, support correspondence, product usage telemetry, and website analytics collected on bratrax.com. That Processing is governed by our Privacy Policy, not by this DPA.

Some Personal Data is Processed in both capacities, for different purposes. The name, email address, and role of Customer's authorized users are Processed:

  • by Bratrax as Processor, where it creates, administers, and removes user accounts, assigns and enforces roles, and records access to Customer Data — all on Customer's instruction and governed by this DPA; and
  • by Bratrax as Controller, where it authenticates those users, bills Customer, sends service and marketing communications, provides support, and measures product usage — purposes Bratrax determines for itself, governed by the Privacy Policy.

This is not a conflict: the same field may be Processed in two capacities where the purposes differ. Where a Data Subject exercises a right in respect of that data, Bratrax will act on Customer's instruction in respect of the Processing described in the first bullet, and will respond directly to the Data Subject in respect of the second.

3. Customer's Instructions and Responsibilities

3.1 Documented instructions

Bratrax will Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law. In that case, Bratrax will inform Customer of the legal requirement before Processing, unless the law prohibits such notice on important grounds of public interest.

The Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's use and configuration of the Service — including which platforms Customer connects, which attribution models Customer selects, which users Customer authorizes, and which optional features Customer enables — constitute Customer's complete documented instructions. Additional or alternative instructions must be agreed in writing and may be subject to additional fees.

3.2 Lawfulness

Customer is responsible for the accuracy, quality, and lawfulness of Customer Personal Data and for the lawfulness of the means by which Customer acquired it. Customer represents that it has provided all required notices, obtained all required consents, and has a valid legal basis for the Processing instructed under this DPA, including for the collection of data through the Bratrax storefront integration on Customer's web properties and for the connection of each Connected Platform.

3.3 Data the Service is not designed for

The Service is built for commerce and advertising data. It is not designed to Process the categories below, and the security measures in Annex II are not calibrated to them.

Customer must not submit to the Service, or connect a data source containing:

  • special categories of Personal Data under Article 9 of the GDPR, including data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic or biometric data, health data, or data concerning sex life or sexual orientation;
  • Personal Data relating to criminal convictions or offences under Article 10 of the GDPR;
  • information protected under HIPAA or comparable health-privacy laws;
  • financial account numbers, payment card numbers, or cardholder data;
  • government-issued identifiers, such as social security, passport, or national insurance numbers; or
  • precise geolocation data gathered for the purpose of locating an individual — for example a feed from a location-tracking service, a mobile device, or telemetry hardware. This does not refer to the coordinates that some commerce platforms attach to a delivery or billing address on an ordinary order record; those are addressed separately below.

Children's data. The Service is not directed to children, and Bratrax does not knowingly collect Personal Data from them. Customer must not use the Service to knowingly Process the Personal Data of children, to target children, or to load or connect a data source consisting of or intended to comprise children's Personal Data. Customer remains responsible for its own obligations under applicable children's privacy law, including any age verification or parental consent requirements that apply to Customer's business.

Address coordinates. Some commerce platforms, including Shopify, include a latitude and longitude alongside the shipping and billing address on an order record. Where a Connected Platform provides them, those coordinates are retrieved and stored as part of that order record. Bratrax does not use them for attribution, does not derive location intelligence from them, and does not expose them as a dimension in any dashboard. They are disclosed here rather than left to be discovered, because they are precise to a delivery address and Customer should account for them in its own record of processing. Bratrax intends to discard these fields at the point of ingestion; until it does, they are held under the same measures as the rest of the order record. Separately, the Bratrax storefront integration never requests location from a visitor's browser and does not use the browser Geolocation API.

Data arriving incidentally. The prohibitions in this Section 3.3 apply to the extent the relevant data is within Customer's control. Where Personal Data of a prohibited category arrives incidentally — through an ordinary synchronisation from a Connected Platform, or through a free-text field completed by Customer's personnel or end users — Customer is not in breach of this Section by reason of that arrival alone. On becoming aware, Customer will take reasonable steps to prevent recurrence and may request Bratrax's assistance in removing the data. Bratrax may suspend an affected data flow where continued Processing would present a legal or security risk.

3.4 Unlawful instructions

Bratrax will inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws. Bratrax may suspend performance of the affected instruction until Customer modifies or confirms it, and is not obliged to carry out an instruction it reasonably believes to be unlawful.

Taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to it, Bratrax will provide reasonable assistance to Customer in:

6. Data Subject Rights

The Service provides Customer with self-service means to access, correct, export, and delete Customer Personal Data, including direct query access to Customer's dedicated data layer. Customer is responsible for responding to Data Subject requests in the first instance.

If Bratrax receives a request from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data, Bratrax will not respond to it directly except to acknowledge receipt and direct the Data Subject to Customer, unless legally required to respond or Customer has authorized Bratrax to do so. Bratrax will notify Customer of the request without undue delay.

Where Customer cannot fulfil a request through the Service, Bratrax will provide reasonable assistance on written request to legal@bratrax.com. Assistance beyond what is reasonably necessary, or which is repetitive or manifestly unfounded, may be subject to a reasonable charge notified to Customer in advance.

7. Personal Data Breach

Bratrax will notify Customer of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Notice will be sent to the email address associated with Customer's account administrator.

The notice will describe, to the extent known at the time and supplemented as further information becomes available: the nature of the breach including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned; the likely consequences; the measures taken or proposed to address it and mitigate its effects; and a contact point for further information.

Bratrax will take reasonable steps to contain and investigate the breach and will provide Customer with reasonable cooperation in meeting Customer's own notification obligations to Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects. Bratrax's notification of, or response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgement of fault or liability.

8. Security Measures

Bratrax's technical and organizational measures are described in Annex II. Two of them are structural rather than merely procedural, and are worth stating in the body of this DPA:

  • Per-customer isolation. Each customer's data is held in a dedicated logical data store. Bratrax does not commingle one customer's data with another's.
  • EU data residency. Customer Personal Data is hosted in the European Union, on dedicated servers in a Hetzner data centre in Helsinki, Finland. Backups are held in the United States, as described in Section 10.1.

9. Audits and Information

Bratrax will make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the GDPR. In the first instance this means the information published in this DPA and its Annexes, together with any security documentation, questionnaire responses, or third-party audit reports Bratrax makes generally available to customers.

Where that information is not sufficient, Customer may request an audit by emailing legal@bratrax.com, subject to the following:

  • audits are conducted during business hours, in a manner that does not disrupt Bratrax's operations, and must not require access to any other customer's data or to Bratrax's premises where that would compromise the security or confidentiality of other customers;

Audit findings are the Confidential Information of both parties.

11. Artificial Intelligence Features

Two distinct data flows involve AI providers, and they carry different responsibilities under this DPA. The distinction is material to a security review, so it is stated here rather than only in the Privacy Policy.

11.1 AI features Bratrax operates

Some Bratrax features generate answers using a large language model — today, the Bratrax assistant for Slack and the in-product AI sidebar. When Customer uses one of these features, the question, the recent context of that conversation, and the Customer Personal Data needed to answer it are transmitted to Anthropic for the sole purpose of generating the response. Anthropic is a Subprocessor listed in Annex III and Processes that data on Bratrax's documented instructions. These features are optional and Process data only when a user invokes them. Where Customer supplies its own Anthropic API key, that traffic runs under Customer's own agreement with Anthropic rather than under this DPA.

11.2 AI providers Customer connects

Customer may connect a third-party AI provider — for example Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT — to the Service through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) or a similar mechanism, using an access token that Customer generates and controls.

When Customer's AI client makes a request, Bratrax authenticates that token, retrieves the requested Customer Personal Data from Customer's dedicated data store, and returns it to that client. Bratrax performs that retrieval and transmission as Customer's Processor, on Customer's documented instruction — in the same manner, and under the same terms of this DPA, as when Customer queries the Service through its dashboard. The difference is the client at the other end of the connection, not the nature of Bratrax's Processing.

The AI provider Customer connects is not a Bratrax Subprocessor. Bratrax does not determine the purposes or means of that provider's Processing, has no contractual relationship with it in respect of Customer Personal Data, and bears no responsibility for its data practices, retention periods, model training policies, or outputs. That provider acts as Customer's own processor or as an independent controller, under Customer's agreement with it.

Customer is responsible for establishing a legal basis for that onward Processing, for its own terms with the provider, and for compliance with any restrictions the provider places on regulated data. Every request in this flow is initiated by Customer's own client; Bratrax transmits nothing on its own initiative. Customer may terminate the connection at any time by regenerating or deleting the access token in the Service, which takes effect immediately.

11.3 Delivery into Slack

The Bratrax assistant for Slack operates in two configurations, and Slack's role differs between them.

Customer's own Slack workspace. Where Customer installs the assistant into a Slack workspace that Customer controls, replies — including any figures, tables, or charts drawn from Customer Personal Data — are delivered into that workspace. Slack Processes that content under Customer's own agreement with Slack, and is not a Bratrax Subprocessor in that flow, on the same basis as Section 11.2. Customer determines who may access the workspace and the linked channel, and may remove the assistant at any time.

The Bratrax community Slack workspace. Bratrax also operates a shared community workspace that Customers may join, in which a channel can be linked to a Customer's account. Because Bratrax operates that workspace under its own agreement with Slack, Slack acts as a Bratrax Subprocessor in respect of content delivered there, and is listed on that basis in Annex III. Replies posted in a linked channel are visible to the members of that channel, and Customer is responsible for controlling channel membership.

In either configuration, Bratrax does not store the content of Slack messages. Message content is Processed in memory to generate a reply. Bratrax stores only the installation record, the granted permission scopes, and any channel link, as described in the Privacy Policy.

11.4 No use of Customer Personal Data to train AI models

Bratrax. Bratrax does not use Customer Personal Data to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop any machine learning or artificial intelligence model. Bratrax does not train machine learning models at all — the attribution models the Service applies are deterministic, rule-based calculations, not learned models.

AI subprocessors. Customer Personal Data transmitted to an AI subprocessor for the features described in Section 11.1 is used solely to generate a response to the requesting user, and is not used to train that provider's models. As at the effective date of this DPA the only such subprocessor is Anthropic, whose commercial terms provide that API inputs and outputs are not used for model training and are retained only for a limited period before deletion. Bratrax will not engage an AI subprocessor that reserves the right to train on Customer Personal Data, and will give notice under Section 5.3 before engaging any new AI subprocessor.

AI providers Customer connects. Where Customer connects its own AI provider under Section 11.2, that provider's training and retention practices are governed by Customer's agreement with it, and Bratrax gives no commitment on its behalf.

Aggregated and de-identified data. Section 13 permits Bratrax to create aggregated and de-identified data that no longer constitutes Personal Data, and to use it to operate, secure, improve, and develop the Service. Where any such use involves model development, it is performed only on data that no longer relates to an identified or identifiable natural person. Bratrax will not use Customer Personal Data in identifiable form for those purposes, will not attempt to re-identify aggregated data, and will not permit any third party to do so.

12. Deletion and Return

On termination or expiry of the Agreement, Bratrax will delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with the following schedule, which mirrors Section 15.4 of the Agreement:

  • Days 0–30: Customer retains access to the Service and to its dedicated data layer and may export Customer Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Days 31–180: Customer Personal Data is held in cold storage, inaccessible to Customer, and may be restored on written request.
  • After 180 days: Bratrax permanently deletes Customer Personal Data.

On Customer's earlier written request to legal@bratrax.com, Bratrax will delete Customer Personal Data within 30 days. A deletion request under this paragraph ends the cold storage window described above and is irreversible. Once deletion has been performed, Customer Personal Data cannot be restored and any later restoration request cannot be met. Customer should export anything it wishes to keep before requesting deletion. This is deliberate: where Customer requests deletion because one of its own Data Subjects has exercised an erasure right, a restorable copy would defeat the request. Bratrax may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law, and where it does so will continue to protect it under this DPA and Process it only for the purpose requiring retention. Backup copies are not deleted individually. They are removed as the backups containing them age out on the rotation schedule in Annex II, which means a copy may persist in backup for up to 90 days after deletion from the live systems. Backups are encrypted and are used only to restore the Service.

On Customer's written request, Bratrax will certify deletion.

13. Aggregated and De-Identified Data

Bratrax creates aggregated and de-identified data from Customer Data in the course of operating the Service. Bratrax performs that de-identification as Customer's Processor and on Customer's instruction. Once data has been aggregated and de-identified such that it no longer relates to an identified or identifiable natural person, it is no longer Personal Data and this DPA does not apply to it.

Bratrax uses aggregated and de-identified data only to operate, secure, improve, and develop the Service. That is a permitted business purpose under the CCPA and is consistent with Bratrax's certification in Section 14.

Bratrax does not currently publish industry benchmarks, and does not use aggregated data in its marketing. Section 5.3 of the Agreement reserves the ability to do so in future. If Bratrax intends to begin either use, it will update this DPA and give notice under Section 16.2 before that use begins, and Customer may opt out as described in the Agreement.

Bratrax will not attempt to re-identify aggregated or de-identified data, and will not permit any Subprocessor or third party to do so.

  • retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Bratrax and Customer; or
  • combine Customer Personal Data with personal information received from, or on behalf of, any other person, or collected from its own interactions with a consumer, except as permitted under the CCPA to perform a business purpose.

Bratrax certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions. Bratrax will notify Customer if it determines it can no longer meet its obligations under the CCPA, and Customer may then take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use.

Each is already provided for elsewhere in this DPA; this Section restates them in the form those statutes require.

  4. Demonstrating compliance. Bratrax will make available to Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with its obligations (Section 9).

Where a state law imposes a mandatory contractual requirement not addressed above, that requirement applies to the extent it is mandatory, and this Section 14.2 is construed to give it effect.

Annex II — Technical and Organizational Measures

This Annex populates Annex II of the SCCs. Bratrax maintains the following measures and may update them provided the overall level of security is not materially reduced.

Pseudonymization and encryption

  • In transit. Connections between Customer and the Service are encrypted using TLS. Traffic between Bratrax's own systems, including between the application layer and the analytical database, travels over an encrypted WireGuard tunnel on a private network and is not exposed to the public internet. Connections to the relational database use TLS, with certificate verification and client certificates on the paths that support it.
  • At rest. Backups are encrypted. Full-disk encryption is not currently enabled on the application servers or on the analytical database, which rely instead on the physical security of the hosting facility, on network isolation, and on the access controls described below. Enabling encryption at rest is on Bratrax's security roadmap. Bratrax states this expressly rather than relying on a general assurance, so that Customer can assess it.
  • OAuth tokens and platform credentials are used only to retrieve the data Customer has authorized, and access to them is restricted to the systems that require it. They are not currently encrypted at the column level; adding application-level encryption for these fields is on Bratrax's security roadmap. Customer may revoke Bratrax's access at any time from the relevant Connected Platform.
  • User passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes and are never stored or transmitted in plaintext.
  • Anonymous visitor activity is keyed to pseudonymous session and device identifiers rather than to directly identifying data, until and unless the visitor is resolved to a known customer through Customer's own data.

Confidentiality and access control

  • Per-customer data isolation. Each customer's data is held in a dedicated logical data store. One customer's data is not commingled with another's, and application-level access controls scope every query to the requesting customer's own data.
  • Role-based access control within the Service: an administrator role with full access to the customer account, and a read-only viewer role that cannot modify configuration, connect platforms, manage users, or access billing.
  • Least-privilege access for Bratrax personnel. Access to production systems and Customer Personal Data is limited to personnel who require it to operate or support the Service.
  • Authentication to the Service is by individual account credentials. Session tokens are JSON Web Tokens signed with an asymmetric key (RS256) and expire 14 days after issue.
  • Personnel are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

Integrity and availability

  • Customer Personal Data is hosted in the European Union, on dedicated servers in a Hetzner data centre in Helsinki, Finland. Backups are held in cloud storage located in the United States.
  • Backups are taken on the following schedule: a weekly full backup of the analytical database retained for 90 days; an hourly incremental backup of the event stream retained for 7 days; and a daily dump of the relational database, of which the 7 most recent copies are kept. All backups are encrypted and are held in cloud storage in the United States, as described in Section 10.1. The backup set comprises the databases only. Application and server logs are not included in it and do not leave the European Union.
  • Data pipeline and data-quality failures — including extraction jobs and per-customer reconciliation — raise automated alerts to the engineering team. Automated alerting on host and infrastructure availability is not currently configured, and is on Bratrax's roadmap.
  • Data retrieved from Connected Platforms is versioned so that corrections and restatements from the source platform are reflected rather than duplicated.

Resilience and restoration

  • The analytics layer is rebuildable from source data held in the raw ingestion layer and, where necessary, by re-retrieval from Connected Platforms, which limits the impact of corruption in derived tables.
  • Backups are taken automatically on the schedule set out above and their creation is monitored. A full restoration from backup has not yet been performed or tested. Establishing a periodic restore test is on Bratrax's roadmap.

Governance and testing

  • Regular review of security practices and of the Subprocessors listed in Annex III.
  • Changes to production systems are made through version-controlled code review.
  • Security incidents are investigated, documented, and remediated, and are notified to affected customers in accordance with Section 7 of this DPA.
  • Bratrax does not currently hold a SOC 2, ISO 27001, or equivalent third-party certification. Where a security certification is required, Customer should evaluate the measures in this Annex directly. Bratrax will update this Annex if that position changes.

Measures applying to Subprocessors

  • Each Subprocessor is engaged under a written contract imposing data protection obligations no less protective, in substance, than those in this DPA.
  • Subprocessors receive only the categories of data necessary for their stated purpose, as set out in Annex III.
  • Subprocessors are reviewed before engagement and periodically thereafter.
