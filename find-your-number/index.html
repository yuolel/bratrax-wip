      What if I disagree with my number?

      Switch it. Three answers get you a sensible starting point, not a verdict. You can change your number any time in Bratrax, and which one you pick tells us something too.

      Do I need a card or a sales call?

      Neither. Connect your store and one ad account to see your number from your own orders. If you want the rest of Bratrax after that, it's $1 for your first month and $99/mo flat after.

      What does Bratrax read?

      Orders from Shopify or WooCommerce, and spend from one ad account to start. Read-only: it doesn't change anything in your store, your ad accounts or your tracking.

      How do you know what a "good" number is?

      We don't pretend to. There's no industry average here. Your number is compared with your own last twelve months, and with a target you set yourself if you want one.

      Why do I have to enter my costs?

      Profit and payback mean nothing without them. Bratrax pulls product costs from your store where it can; you confirm shipping and payment fees. If you don't know your product costs by item, one percentage of sales is enough to start.
