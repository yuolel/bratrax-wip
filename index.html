05 Opinions

Bratrax has opinions. You're welcome.

Most analytics tools hand you a blank canvas and two hundred settings. Bratrax ships with the decisions already made, the way a good CFO would make them. Change a default when you have a reason.

  1. Profit dollars are the score.ROAS and margin % explain it. They don't replace it.
  2. New customers are bought. Returning ones are earned.So every number is split in two, and judged differently.
  3. Attribution steers. It doesn't keep score.It tells you where the next dollar goes. Not whether you made money.
  4. Every miss is volume or efficiency.Too few customers, or too expensive ones. Nothing else.
  5. Defaults, not settings.Connect your store and start. Tweak later, if ever.

08 A note from the founder

Why we stopped being an attribution tool.

From:Brat Vukovich, founder
To:Every D2C team with a Monday meeting
Re:One number

I built Bratrax as an attribution tool. It got good at telling you where a sale came from. It still couldn't tell you whether the sale made money.

And that's the question that matters. Today every seat in a D2C company runs on a different number. The founder watches revenue. Finance sees profit when the books close, weeks later. Media buyers optimize to the ROAS the platforms report about themselves. Creative judges ads by hook rate. So nobody can say whether a marketing decision helped the business.

"Brands don't want another tool to set up. They want an answer."

So Bratrax starts from profit now. Profit dollars are the score. ROAS, MER and cost per customer explain the score. They don't replace it. Attribution still lives inside, and it still matters, but its job is to steer the next dollar, not to keep score.

What you get is one number your brand runs on, and a straight answer every morning: is it moving the right way, and what do we change?

— Brat@bratinceptly

09 Questions

Fair questions.

What does "digital CFO" actually mean?

It means Bratrax does the part of a CFO's job that's about marketing: deciding which numbers matter for your brand, and telling you every day whether they're moving the right way. It doesn't do your books, taxes or payroll. Keep your accountant.

Isn't this just another attribution tool?

No. Attribution is inside, down to the single ad, and it's good at telling you where the next dollar should go. It can't tell you whether to spend that dollar at all. That question starts with profit, and that's where Bratrax starts.

How does Bratrax know which number is mine?

It depends on how your brand makes money. Brands that live on repeat orders and subscriptions run on payback. Brands spending hard to grow run on new-customer cost. Most others run on profit per order. MER sits on top of all three, and you can switch any time you disagree with us.

How does the $1 first month work?

When you're in, you pay $1 to start. For your first month, run Bratrax beside whatever you use today, whether that's an analytics tool, a profit app or a spreadsheet. You don't have to switch yet. If by your renewal date Bratrax does the job as well or better, you keep us at the rate shown on the pricing card. If not, cancel from settings before your renewal date and you pay nothing more.

When does my trial end?

Your first month is $1. After that, your subscription renews at $99/mo on your monthly anniversary — the same day of the next month you signed up. We'll send you a renewal reminder 7 days before, so you have a full week to decide. Cancel from settings in one click if you want out. No questions, no follow-up.

What if Bratrax isn't for me?

You cancel from settings in one click before your renewal date and you pay nothing more. The $1 you paid to run us beside whatever you're using is non-refundable — that's the price of the comparison. No follow-up, no win-back email, no sales call. We'd rather you cancel cleanly than stay because canceling was friction.

Lite or Clear Vision?

Lite is the software: $99/mo, self-serve, no contract. Clear Vision is the operator edition: the same software plus a dedicated analyst attached to your account, weekly reviews and a custom data model, at $5,000/mo. Most brands start with Lite.

How fresh is my data?

Your store's orders refresh every hour. Ad platforms, email and SMS refresh every 4 hours — that's the platforms' own reporting rhythm, and spend figures firm up over the day on their side too. If a number looks stale, every dashboard shows its “as of” time.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude at launch — install the MCP endpoint with your own Anthropic API key. OpenAI on roadmap. In Slack, type @bratrax and the answer lands in the thread, included with every plan. If you don't use either, the dashboards still stand alone.
