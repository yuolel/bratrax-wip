Everyone on your team is reading a different number.

Nobody's wrong. That's the problem. Four numbers, four meetings, and still no answer to the only question that matters: did last week's spend make money?

And underneath all four, your attribution tool files about a fifth of revenue under "Direct." That's not a channel. It's the tool's polite way of saying it doesn't know.

02 The idea

Fifteen metrics. You need one.

A good CFO doesn't hand you forty charts. They tell you which number to watch and whether it's moving the right way. Bratrax works the same way, from your own orders.

11 Questions

Common questions.

Is Bratrax an attribution tool?

Attribution is inside it. It's how we get the numbers right, and it reads the order, not just the pixel, so less of your revenue lands in "Direct." But attribution is one input, not the point. The point is the number you run the business on: profit per order, new-customer cost or payback, with MER on top.

How is Bratrax different from Triple Whale, Northbeam or Hyros?

They're attribution tools. Bratrax has attribution inside, but it's built around the one number you run the business on, read from your own orders. It's also $99/mo flat at any GMV, with no contract and no demo call, and your data connects to the AI you already pay for.

How does the $1 first month work?

When you're in, you pay $1 to start. For your first month, run Bratrax beside whatever you use today, whether that's an attribution tool, a profit app or a spreadsheet. You don't have to switch yet. If by your renewal date Bratrax does the job as well or better, you keep us at the rate shown on the pricing card. If not, cancel from settings before your renewal date and you pay nothing more.

When does my trial end?

Your first month is $1. After that, your subscription renews at $99/mo on your monthly anniversary — the same day of the next month you signed up. We'll send you a renewal reminder 7 days before, so you have a full week to decide. Cancel from settings in one click if you want out. No questions, no follow-up.

What if Bratrax doesn't work for me?

You cancel from settings in one click before your renewal date and you pay nothing more. The $1 you paid to run us beside whatever you're using is non-refundable — that's the price of the comparison. No follow-up, no win-back email, no sales call. We'd rather you cancel cleanly than stay because canceling was friction.

What's the difference between Lite and Clear Vision?

Lite is the software: $99/mo, self-serve, no contract. Clear Vision is the operator edition: the same software plus a dedicated analyst attached to your account, weekly reviews and a custom data model, at $5,000/mo. Most brands start with Lite.

How fresh is my data?

Your store's orders refresh every hour. Ad platforms, email and SMS refresh every 4 hours — that's the platforms' own reporting rhythm, and spend figures firm up over the day on their side too. If a number looks stale, every dashboard shows its “as of” time.

Which AI assistants does Bratrax connect to?

Claude at launch — install the MCP endpoint with your own Anthropic API key. OpenAI on roadmap. In Slack, type @bratrax and the answer lands in the thread, included with every plan. If you don't use either, the dashboards still stand alone.

How much can you actually shrink the "Direct" bucket?

Depends on your stack. The mechanism: our tracking pixel captures the on-site journey, and your store's order record acts as ground truth — when the pixel misses something, the order itself usually has enough context to recover the source. We've gotten it to 3% on a Bratrax client. Another landed at 5%. We won't promise a number because the result depends on conditions we don't control.

Will this give me "perfect" attribution?

No. No tool can. Attribution is correlation, not causation — Bratrax won't tell you why a customer bought, but it will show you what they actually did on your site: every session, every source, every touch we can recover. The picture is honest. The judgment is yours.
